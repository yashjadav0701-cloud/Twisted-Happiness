No-Return Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

Why handmade products are generally non-returnable

Every Twisted Happiness creation is handmade or prepared specifically for your confirmed request. Significant time, skill, materials and studio capacity are committed before your product is ready. Small variations in colour, texture, shape or placement are natural characteristics of handmade work and are not defects.

For these reasons, products are generally non-returnable and non-exchangeable after the order details have been confirmed and work has begun.

Issues we will review

We will fairly review a request when you receive:

  • a product damaged during delivery;
  • a product different from the item confirmed on WhatsApp; or
  • a product with a material difference from the final customisation details that the studio explicitly confirmed.

How to report a genuine issue

Contact us on WhatsApp within 24 hours of delivery. Please keep the original packaging and send:

  • your enquiry or order reference;
  • clear photos of the product and packaging;
  • a short unedited opening video, where available; and
  • a clear explanation of the issue.

Do not dispose of the product or packaging until the studio has reviewed the evidence.

Resolution

After verification, Twisted Happiness may offer the most appropriate solution: repair guidance, replacement of the affected item, correction of the confirmed customisation, store credit, or a refund for the affected product. The solution depends on the nature of the issue and the availability of materials.

What is not covered

  • change of mind, personal preference or gifting-plan changes;
  • minor handmade variations or slight colour differences caused by screen settings and lighting;
  • damage caused after delivery through water, heat, pressure, rough handling, incorrect storage or normal wear;
  • incorrect details supplied or approved by the customer; or
  • delivery delays caused by incomplete address information, unavailable recipients, courier disruptions or events outside the studio’s reasonable control.

Cancellation

A cancellation request may be considered only before materials have been allocated or crafting has started. Once work begins, cancellation may not be possible. Any approved cancellation and refund method will be confirmed through WhatsApp.

Need help?

Our goal is to resolve legitimate issues thoughtfully and fairly. Contact the studio through the WhatsApp link on the website and include your reference.
